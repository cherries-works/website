Guard and Deploy are no longer maintained

Guard and Deploy are no longer actively maintained. As projects grow and priorities change, some projects eventually have to be put aside to make room for others.

What This Means

Both projects will be taken down.

Because these projects were neither maintained, or used, and making the source-code available was a big factor, on why Pulse was held back.

If you are currently using either project, you can continue doing so, but keep in mind that they are no longer maintained.

Why They Are Being Discontinued

Maintaining multiple projects takes time, and spreading that time across everything does not always make sense.

Rather than continuing to maintain projects that we can no longer give enough attention to, we have decided to focus on the projects that are currently moving forward.

This does not mean that Guard and Deploy were pointless projects. They were part of the process of building cherries.works and learning what works best.

Looking Forward

cherries.works is still actively developing new ideas and improving existing projects.

For now, the focus is on projects that can receive proper attention, useful updates, and long-term maintenance.

Thank you to everyone who tried Guard or Deploy, reported issues, or contributed to their development.
