About Us

cherries.works is an idea of mine that I had a while ago.

My LapTop is pretty beefy, but I had a LapTop previously that was not so efficient. And it was very annoying to me, whenever programms would run very slowly, and eat away all of my RAM and CPU.

Jump forward 3 years, I learned a lot about Low-Level programming, and want to use what I learned for good.

My goal with cherries.works is to provide software that is memory efficient, and simply works. Software that is open-source, and trustworthy. Software that would have respected my old LapTop.

Now, I am not trying to replace everything, that would be crazy. I am trying to see what tools I could re-create quickly, and very effectively. cherries.works is not trying to re-invent the wheel.

cherries.works simply wants to make programs optimized again.
